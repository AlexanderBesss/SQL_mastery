What this demo is — and is not

The graph is a hand-authored visualization of this report's argument. It does not parse markdown, run a graph database, or query an index. For the real pattern, point ki at a folder of markdown, or use Obsidian's graph view as a human map. The point here is the shape: a small graph you can actually read, with pages as entry points and evidence as leaves.
